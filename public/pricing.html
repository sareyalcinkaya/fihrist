FAQ

Common questions.

Is there really no sales call required?
Correct. The Free and Starter tiers are self-serve from day one. For Growth, just sign up and get started. Institution tier involves a short onboarding conversation — that's it.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Data is always preserved.
What counts as an AI screening credit?
One credit = one full applicant screened (CV, transcript, and motivation letter). The Growth tier has unlimited credits.
We're a small student club with no budget. Is the free tier genuinely free?
Yes. No credit card, no trial period. Free permanently for up to 2 programmes and 50 applicants per cycle.
