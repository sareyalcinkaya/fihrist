How it works

From application to programme complete.

Five stages. Every manual process removed from the week.

01
Intake

Build a form once. Applicants submit through a clean portal. Frist.ai creates a folder per applicant automatically.

02
Screen

AI reads every document, scores applicants against the criteria, and surfaces the strongest candidates.

03
Decide

Review scored shortlists. Accept, reject, or waitlist with one click. Emails go out automatically.

04
Run

Track attendance, milestones, and flag participants at risk before the problem compounds.

05
Report

One-click outcome reports for boards, funders, and CSRD/ESG compliance.
