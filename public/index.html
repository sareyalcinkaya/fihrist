01 / The Problem
"Every application round, I spend two weeks doing things a computer could do in two minutes."

Programme coordinators are the backbone of Europe's innovation ecosystem. They run incubators, fellowships, and accelerators that launch hundreds of startups every year. And they do it with Google Sheets, shared inboxes, and sheer determination.

The tools built for them either don't exist, cost more than their annual budget, or were designed for a different industry entirely.

  • Sorting 300 applications by hand, copy-pasting eligibility checks into spreadsheets
  • Chasing missing documents over email for weeks before a decision can even begin
  • No real-time view of cohort health — only the coordinator knows what's happening
  • Board reporting requires building a new spreadsheet from scratch every cycle
  • Submittable and AcceleratorApp cost €800+/month and require a sales call
  • Student clubs and small university programmes are completely unserved
02 / How It Works

From first application
to programme complete.

Five stages. Every manual process removed from your week.

01
Intake
Custom application forms

Build your form once. Applicants submit documents, CVs, and motivation letters through a clean portal. Fihrist creates a folder per applicant automatically.

02
Screen
AI eligibility scoring

Fihrist reads every document. It checks eligibility criteria, scores motivation letters, flags red flags, and surfaces the strongest candidates — before you open a single file.

03
Decide
Review & accept

Your team reviews scored shortlists, not raw piles. Accept, reject, or waitlist with one click. Emails go out automatically, on your template.

04
Run
Cohort management

Track attendance, send check-ins, monitor milestones, and flag participants at risk of dropping before the problem becomes a problem.

05
Report
Outcomes & insights

One-click outcome reports for boards, funders, and CSRD/ESG compliance. Every cycle builds your programme's evidence base.
